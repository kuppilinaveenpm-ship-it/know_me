Consultant · Product & Project Manager · Business Analyst · AI Builder

I’m a technology professional with 20+ years of experience across business analysis, solution design, project delivery and product transformation, mainly in insurance and financial services, and increasingly at the intersection of AI, governance and business strategy. A big part of my work has been understanding business problems, asking the right questions, working out practical solutions, and bringing business and technology teams together to make them happen. I’ve worked across insurance core systems, digital transformation, integrations, automation, data, AML-CFT, customer experience and, more recently, AI.

Depending on what a project needs, I’m comfortable getting into the detail myself by analysing a problem, working through requirements, designing a solution or figuring out how something should work. I’m equally comfortable stepping back, coordinating different teams, resolving issues and keeping things moving towards delivery. That mix of hands-on problem solving and working across teams has shaped how I approach transformation.

I tend to look at technology through a business-value lens. It’s not just about what a system can do, but what it can do for the organisation, its customers, operations, efficiency, products and future potential. That’s the lens I bring to an AI opportunity, a business problem or a legacy system that has become a bottleneck. I’m interested in where technology can genuinely improve the way a business works, rather than using technology simply because it is available.

I’m still a DIY guy at heart. I build terrariums and RC vehicles when time permits. I like figuring things out myself, and I build the AI tools I advise people to think about, often experimenting with them in the evenings. It keeps my thinking grounded. If I can’t get an AI agent to work reliably myself, I’m certainly not going to tell a client that it’s ready to run their operations. I often write on LinkedIn about Business Analysis, project delivery, AI, governance and the practical side of using technology to create business value.
